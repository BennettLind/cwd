Global Markets Program

This program allows NFL teams to gain international marketing rights in countries outside of the U.S. The program's main purpose is to build the NFL brand globally and grow fans around the world. The GMP, as of March 2026, includes all 32 teams across 22 countries, which allows those teams to essentially become a monopoly, with some teams giving rights to more than one country. This helps sell that team's merchandise, viewership, and revenue. With the Olympics coming to the United States, this program also allows for the sport to grow and bring more athletes.
